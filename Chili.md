# Chili


Preparation: 
## Ingredients 
* 3lb Chuck Roast
* 6 tablespoons olive oil or coconut oil
* 3 medium onions chopped
* 8 cloves garlic chopped
* 1 jalapeno pepper seeded and de-ribbed, finely chopped
* 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour or gluten free all-purpose
* 1/4 cup chili powder (about 4 oz mixed dried chilis)
* 1 1/2 tablespoons ground cumin
* 1 tablespoon ground oregano
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1/8 teaspoon cloves, grounded
* 4 cups beef broth
* 1 28 ounce can plum tomatoes drained and chopped
* 1 28 ounce canc rushed tomatoes
* 2 chipotle chilis in adobo sauce, sliced
* 1 T adobo sauce
* 1 15 ounce can black beans
* 1 15 ounce can small red kidney beans
* 1 ounce unsweetened dark baker's chocolate, grated

## Materials
* Smoker
* Big Pot/dutch oven

## Method
* Smoke the chuck roast & dice into 1 in cubes (add them in later) or cut chuck roast into 1 in cubes, brown with 3-4 T oil and remove from the pot. Leave the fond.
* Add 2 more tablespoons of oil and cook the onions, jalapenos and garlic until softened, about 2 or 3 minutes. Return the browned meat to the pot (if smoking, put it in when it is ready).
* Sprinkle the meat and vegetable mixture with the flour and stir and cook for 1 minute.
* Add the chili powder, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, chipotles, adobo, tomatoes and beef broth.
* Simmer partially covered over low heat for about 1 to 1 1/2 hours or until the meat is tender and the chili has cooked down. You want it thick but not soupy. Stir it occasionally. It will probably stick a bit on the bottom and that's to be expected. Just make sure your heat isn't on too high and use a diffuser underneath your pot if you have one. (See Kate's Tips for the one I use.)
* Open your 2 cans of beans, drain them and add them to the chili. Grate the chocolate and add it as well. Taste and adjust seasonings.
* Serve garnished with Monterey Jack or cheddar cheese, chopped cilantro, fresh sour cream, chopped onion, chopped avocado, chopped jalapenos and tortilla chips
